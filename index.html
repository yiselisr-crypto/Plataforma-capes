Estrato distribution

Coverage by research area

How estratos are assigned

For journals, the estrato shown is the one computed from the Scopus highest-category percentile, following the CAPES Procedure 2 bands (A1 ≥ 87.5%, A2 ≥ 75%, A3 ≥ 62.5%, A4 ≥ 50%, A5 ≥ 37.5%, A6 ≥ 25%, A7 ≥ 12.5%, A8 < 12.5%). For conferences, the estrato comes from the curated CE-SBC classification (H5-index based, with Top10/Top20 adjustment).

04 / Metrics

Output selection and weighting

Parameters

N = average number of permanent faculty members (excluding PPJ and PPS).

M = max(10, N/4).

4N = bibliographic output limit.

4N selection

  • Top 4N articles
  • Books (L1, L2, L3)
  • Book chapters (CL1)

M selection

  • Top M technical outputs
  • Tiers T1, T2 and T3

Bibliographic output

ProdBib = #A1×1.000 + #A2×0.875 + #A3×0.750 + #A4×0.625
+ #L1×1.0 + #L2×0.8 + #L3×0.6 + #CL1×0.5

Technical output

ProdTec = #T1×1.0 + #T2×0.8 + #T3×0.6

Weighting

Weighted score = Total / N

Qualitative adjustment

The base rating may be adjusted by the evaluation field based on:

  • Relevant technical output
  • Balanced distribution among faculty members
  • Student participation in outputs
  • Consistent output throughout the four-year period
